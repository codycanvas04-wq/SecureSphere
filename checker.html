🧩 Password checklist

  • ○ At least 12 characters
  • ○ 16 or more characters
  • ○ Uses more than one character type
  • ○ Avoids common or predictable patterns
  • ○ Avoids repeated characters and sequences
💡 Tip: A long, unique passphrase made from unrelated words can be easier to remember and harder to guess.
