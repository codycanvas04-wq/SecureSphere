WELCOME TO THE THREAT DETECTOR

Think Before You Click!

Not every message, link or online offer is trustworthy. Learn to spot warning signs and protect your personal information from common online scams.

👾 Meet the Digital Trouble-Makers

🎣

Phishing Messages

Fake emails or texts try to trick you into sharing passwords, bank details or verification codes.

🎁

Fake Prizes

A message claims you won a prize, then asks for money or sensitive information.

🎭

Impersonation

A scammer pretends to be a friend, bank employee, delivery agent or support representative.

⏰

Urgency Tricks

Threatening messages demand immediate action so you panic instead of checking the facts.

🚩 Warning Signs to Look For

🔍 Check the message carefully

  • Unexpected requests for passwords, OTPs or payment.
  • Threats that your account will close immediately.
  • Prize claims that seem too good to be true.
  • Unfamiliar or misspelled website addresses.
  • Unexpected attachments or links.
  • Requests to install unknown apps.

Remember: A message can look professional and still be fraudulent.

🛡️ Your 4-Step Safety Shield

  1. Pause: Don't act under pressure.
  2. Inspect: Check the sender and website address.
  3. Verify: Use the organisation's official app or website.
  4. Protect: Never share passwords, OTPs or recovery codes.

🆘 What if you already clicked?

Don't panic. Close the suspicious page. If you shared a password, change it through the official service and anywhere else you reused it. Enable two-factor authentication where available.

If you suspect financial fraud in India, contact your bank promptly and report it through the official helpline 1930 or the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.
