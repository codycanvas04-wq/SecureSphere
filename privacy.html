Your Data, Your Rules!

Your name, phone number, photos, location and online activity can reveal a lot about you. Learn how to share less, check your settings and protect your digital life.

🌈 What Should You Protect?

🪪

Personal details

Avoid publicly sharing your home address, phone number, identity documents or other private information.

📍

Your location

Review which apps can access your location. Allow it only when needed and choose limited access where possible.

📸

Photos and posts

Think before posting. Photos may reveal your school, workplace, daily routine or other identifying details.

📲

App permissions

Check whether an app really needs access to your camera, microphone, contacts or photos.

🎭 Everyday Privacy Scenarios

Scenario 1: A quiz asks for your birthday 🎂

A social media quiz asks for your full name, birthday, school and your first pet's name to show you a fun result.

Think: Does this quiz genuinely need all that information?

Safer choice: Skip unnecessary questions. Some answers can also reveal details used in account recovery or security questions.

Scenario 2: An app wants every permission 📱

A simple wallpaper app requests access to your contacts, microphone and precise location.

Think: Are these permissions necessary for its main purpose?

Safer choice: Deny permissions that aren't needed. Review the app's privacy information and settings.

Scenario 3: A stranger asks for an OTP 🔔

Someone claiming to be a support agent asks you to share a one-time password sent to your phone.

Think: Should you share the code to prove your identity?

Safer choice: Never share your OTP or sign-in verification code. Contact the service using its official app or website if you are unsure.

🚨 What If Your Personal Information Is Exposed?

A data leak, stolen phone, hacked account, or accidental share can put your personal information at risk. Don't panic! Take these steps to reduce the damage.

🔐

1. Change Exposed Passwords

Change the affected password immediately. If you reused it on other accounts, change those passwords too. Use a different, strong password for every account.

🛡️

2. Secure Your Accounts

Turn on two-factor authentication, sign out unfamiliar sessions, and check your recovery email address and phone number for unexpected changes.

👀

3. Check for Suspicious Activity

Review recent logins, messages, transactions, and account changes. Contact your bank immediately if financial details may have been exposed.

📣

4. Report the Incident

Report the problem to the affected platform using its official website or app. In India, you can report cybercrime through the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.

Visit the Cyber Crime Reporting Portal ↗
📱

5. Protect Your Phone and SIM

If your phone is lost, use its official device-finding service to lock it or erase it when appropriate. Contact your mobile operator if your SIM may have been stolen or misused.

🧾

6. Keep Evidence

Save relevant screenshots, suspicious messages, emails, transaction details, and complaint reference numbers. Never share your password or OTP while reporting.

⚠️ Remember: If you lose money through cyber fraud in India, call 1930 promptly and report it through the official cybercrime portal. Never pay someone who promises to recover your money for a fee.
